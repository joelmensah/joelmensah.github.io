EDEXCEL MARK SCHEME CODES
M (Method): Awarded for a correct method or partial method.
P (Process): Awarded for a correct problem-solving process.
A (Accuracy): Awarded for the correct answer following M/P.
B (Independent): Unconditional accuracy mark (no method needed).
C (Communication): Awarded for a correct mathematical statement/reason.
